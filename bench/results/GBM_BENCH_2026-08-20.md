# gbm-bench covtype, 2026-08-20, and why the whole board moved

`bench/results/gbm_bench_covtype_2026-08-20_113845.json`, NVIDIA's gbm-bench
run unmodified through `bench/external/run_gbm_bench.sh covtype 100 full`, all
six arms interleaved in one process on a quiet M4 on AC power. The extension
was rebuilt at `9f196f9` first, because the previous run predates the
packed-bins wiring and the `.so` on disk was older than four source files.

## The numbers, against 2026-08-18

| arm | 08-20 | 08-18 | delta | accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| xgb-cpu | 7.30 | 12.15 | -39.9% | 0.8560 |
| lgbm-cpu | 8.05 | 8.96 | -10.2% | 0.8849 |
| lgbm-cpu-det | 8.28 | 10.86 | -23.7% | 0.8849 |
| cat-cpu | 9.36 | 17.99 | -48.0% | 0.7948 |
| mojotrees-cpu | 21.62 | 25.40 | -14.9% | 0.8832 |
| mojotrees-gpu | 40.55 | 46.11 | -12.1% | 0.8804 |

## The delta column is not a result and reading it as one is the trap

Every arm got faster, including three libraries this repository has not
touched in any way and did not rebuild. CatBoost gained 48 percent and
XGBoost 40 percent between two runs of identical binaries. Nothing we did
caused that. The 08-18 run was taken in a worse machine window, and an
absolute time carried across two days measures the box.

**The ratios are the result, and they did not move.**

| ratio | 08-20 | 08-18 |
|---|---|---|
| our CPU behind lgbm-cpu | 2.69x | 2.83x |
| our GPU behind our own CPU | 1.88x | 1.82x |
| our GPU behind lgbm-cpu | 5.04x | 5.15x |

Two independent days, two thermal windows, same three ratios. That agreement
is worth more than either day's absolute numbers, and it is the reason arms
are interleaved in one process rather than compared across runs.

## What this says about the packed-bins work

Packed bins was wired on 2026-08-19 in `6dbfc12` after `9126867` found it
inert on the training path. On covtype it buys nothing measurable: the
GPU-to-CPU ratio is 1.88x today against 1.82x two days ago, which is a small
move in the wrong direction inside the noise. The change is not a regression
and it is not a win. It is not visible here.

## Accuracy is unchanged and remains the honest part

0.8832 CPU and 0.8804 GPU against LightGBM's 0.8849, so we give up 0.17 and
0.45 accuracy points respectively. CatBoost at 0.7948 is far behind on this
dataset at 100 trees and its speed should be read with that in mind.

## Publication

Nothing here is publishable as it stands. One run of each arm is not a
measurement, and `bench/external/README.md` requires repeats. What two runs
do support is the negative claim above: no measurable change from the
packed-bins round on this dataset.
